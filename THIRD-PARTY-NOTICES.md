## Additional scientific reference facts

- Standard atomic weights: [CIAAW 2024](https://ciaaw.org/atomic-weights.htm), with original intervals and uncertainty notation.
- Isotope masses and compositions: [NIST SRD 144](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Compositions/index.html), published evaluation cited in snapshot metadata.
- Nuclear properties: F. G. Kondev et al., “The NUBASE2020 evaluation of nuclear physics properties,” Chinese Physics C 45, 030001 (2021); [AMDC data](https://www-nds.iaea.org/amdc/ame2020/nubase_4.mas20.txt). Source edition and retrieval hashes are recorded.
- Oxidation states, discovery attribution, radius and affinity: [PubChem periodic table](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/periodic-table/).

Scientific facts are transformed into the application's attributed snapshot; upstream website HTML and scripts are not shipped. Retrieval is not independent scientific review.
